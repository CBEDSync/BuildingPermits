DBP · System of Systems — The Bicycle

The DBP System of Systems, as a Bicycle

Each part of the digital building permit is a component of the bike; the frame tubes and chain are the relationships that connect them. A bike only rolls when every part is present and linked. Click a part or a link to see what it does for the DBP and the papers behind it. This uses a revised 6-part model — see the note below.

Note on the parts: a proposed re-grouping into six — Data and Models (pedals), Tools and Approaches (drivetrain), Regulations and Compliance (rear wheel), Governance and Processes (front wheel), People and Capabilities (rider) and Purposes and Values (steering). Purposes and Values is the handlebars — it sets the direction the permit serves (sustainability, housing, productivity, wellbeing), and Governance and Processes is the front wheel it steers. People and Capabilities (skills, training, staffing) is often secondary — touched by 27 of the 105 papers on full-text reading, but rarely their main focus. The name “Purposes and Values” was previously “Purpose & Collective Impact”.
